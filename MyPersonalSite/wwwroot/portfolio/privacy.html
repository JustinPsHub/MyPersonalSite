Privacy & analytics.

Optional analytics helps me understand how this portfolio is discovered and used. It starts only after you choose to allow it. You can change your choice below. Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track disable collection.

What is collected

Page paths, relevant demonstration and navigation actions, résumé link clicks, contact actions, foreground engagement, device and browser categories, viewport sizes, referring domain, limited campaign labels, supported browser performance measurements, and redacted error categories. Cloudflare may supply approximate country, region and city based on the connection. These are estimates, not identity.

Random browser identifiers expire after 24 hours; per-tab sessions expire after 30 minutes without activity. Optional analytics uses browser storage after consent. Your preference is saved for up to 180 days. No advertising cookies, cross-site identity matching, session recordings or fingerprinting are used.

What is not collected

No names, email messages, contact contents, passwords, authentication tokens, raw IP addresses in analytics records, exact location, full referring URLs, sensitive query strings, search terms, exception messages or stack traces. An anonymous visit cannot establish someone’s real identity, employer or occupation.

Storage and retention

Usage events are processed by Cloudflare through the portfolio’s analytics service. Detailed events and journeys are available for 30 days; aggregate counts for 365 days; administrator audit counts for 90 days. Cleanup is automatic when the service receives traffic, so physical deletion may lag during inactivity. Brief daily keyed source hashes throttle collector abuse and are not displayed as visitor identities.

Azure hosts the public website and contact API. Hosting and identity providers may maintain their own operational or security logs under their policies. The private dashboard is restricted to the owner. A résumé file click does not prove a completed download; a contact action does not prove an email was sent.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the protected contact page. Turning analytics off removes optional identifiers in this browser; aggregate counts cannot identify an individual.
